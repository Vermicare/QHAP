# QHAP Advance-1000 — Evidence-first research checkpoint

**Date:** 2026-10-27. **Evidence status:** Offline executed host simulation, NOT production mining improvement. **Hardware touched:** None. No Bitaxe/ESP32/Pico/Pi firmware, voltage, clock, pool, or wiring changed.

## Question and test design
An aggressive "advance 1000" request was interpreted as **systematically testing 1,024 software/controller policies** plus **1,024 speculative cryptographic gates**, not as claiming a 1,000x speedup.

The standalone Python reproducer and raw JSON/CSV + SHA256 digest manifest were supplied as conversation artifacts in `QHAP_ADVANCE1000_RESEARCH_BUNDLE.zip`; they were **not automatically uploaded to GitHub**. This checkpoint records the measured host-run outputs. Reproducibility requires retrieving that bundle and rerunning with NumPy.

1. Full factorial 4^5 (1,024) over pool recovery, stale-work refresh, nonce deduplication, thermal guard and power recovery. Common synthetic random draws, seeded `202610271000`. All losses, recoveries, penalties, thermal effects, and sensor estimates are hypothetical, NOT Gamma-calibrated.
2. Training 12,000 synthetic draws across four regimes, validation 12,000 fresh draws; 64 finalists selected from train and final best picked on validation; 84,000 evaluation draws across six regimes, including two deliberately withheld difficult regimes.
3. Winning fixed policy ID **979**: pool recovery 1, stale refresh 1, dedup 1/3, thermal guard 0, power recovery 1. **Not deployable as-is:** real alternative share-accepting pool route and power backup hardware are unverified; stock AxeOS may already avoid these faults.
4. Separate threshold-gated proposal `QHAP-SHIELD`, nominal trigger at 0.3% observed proxy loss, chosen on simulation train; not deployed.
5. Exactly 1,048,576 **real SHA256d evaluations** on 80-byte Bitcoin-shaped candidate headers with toy target "final digest first byte = 00"; winner count **4,174** unchanged under linear, odd-stride and shuffled permutations of same complete nonce set. No enhancement from nonce ordering.
6. Separate evaluation of 1,024 first-SHA256-parity gate hypotheses from those real outputs: train 297/518 winning toy hashes retained; validation 152/279; untouched test **127/249**. Toy useful-hits-per-idealized-compression on untouched test **0.6127x** baseline, thus **WORSE**, not a true cryptographic optimization. No conditional gate may discard actual network-winning hashes without proving it lossless.

**Verdict:** This is a negative safety challenge. No control policy is approved for firmware integration or physical actuation. It shows that blindly enabling features reduces performance when faults are absent or other costs dominate. A toy oracle erasing all modeled remediable combined-stress losses has only +14.655% median improvement; its assumptions are not validated.

## The 1,000x ambition: quantitative reality check
At frozen reference difficulty D=140,577,680,051,919.59, historical H=1.337 TH/s, subsidy=3.125 BTC and assumed 2% fee:
- Historic mathematical expectation 0.000000585929 BTC/day; this is **not** a solo-mining daily payout.
- Literal **1,000x valid hash rate** = **1.337 PH/s**; expected subsidy ~0.000585929 BTC/day; probability of at least one block within a year ~6.745% at frozen difficulty. One thousand times faster remains far below expected 1 BTC/day.
- **One BTC/day long-run expected subsidy** requires ~**2.28185 EH/s**, about **1.7067 million** of the historical Gamma equivalent at the fixed difficulty. Network difficulty changes and transaction fees matter.

The probability formula is `P >= 1 block by t = 1-exp(-H_effective*t/(D*2^32))`. No known search-order permutation, opaque AI scoring, power-system optimization or unproved SHA intermediate predictor changes success probability of a fixed independent SHA256d trial.
